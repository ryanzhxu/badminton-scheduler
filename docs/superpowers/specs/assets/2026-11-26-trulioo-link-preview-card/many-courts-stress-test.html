Same three options, now with 5 courts / 20 players

The sample roster in the last round was small enough to be forgiving. Here's what happens at a more typical "full gym" night — 20 players, 5 courts, round 1 of 10 — and how each layout would need to adapt.
Option A Lineup ticket
Holds up well
Adaptation rule: Court 1 stays big and full-name (that slot never shrinks, regardless of court count). The side list caps at 2 more courts as short one-line rows, then collapses the rest into a plain count — "+2 more courts" — rather than cramming 5 matchups into a shrinking font. Roster avatars already had an overflow affordance ("+N"), so they don't need to change at all.

Option B Scoreboard split
Needs a hard cap
Adaptation rule: cap the side-by-side row at 3 court blocks, full stop — a 4th slot becomes a "+2 courts" tile instead of shrinking every block to fit. Shrinking the blocks to literally fit 5 courts was tried and rejected here: names drop below ~11px and the "glance at it" premise breaks. Capping preserves the bold, legible feel this option is for, at the cost of not showing every court.

Option C Program sheet
Scales, but gets dense fast
Adaptation rule: it's already just a list, so it naturally grows — 5 court rows fit by shrinking row height and font a bit further than the 2-court version. 20 names in the roster column already pushes two-column wrapping to its limit; past roughly 24 players it would need a third column or a "+N" cutoff like the other options use for roster. This is the option where "more courts" costs legibility gradually rather than hitting a hard wall, but it was already the least legible option at small size, and this makes that worse.
